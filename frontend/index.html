Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about fresh milk subscriptions.

How does the morning and evening milk delivery work?
Local dairies dispatch their delivery riders in dedicated time slots (usually 5:30 AM – 7:30 AM for morning and 5:30 PM – 7:30 PM for evening). Your fresh milk is placed right at your doorstep in hygienic glass or food-grade containers.
Can I pause my subscription when going on vacation?
Yes! You have full flexibility. From your Customer Dashboard, simply click "Pause Subscription" before 9:00 PM the previous night. No charges will be applied for paused days.
Is the milk unadulterated and tested?
Yes, all partner dairies on Near Dairy are vetted for FSSAI compliance and zero-chemical handling. Fat % and SNF % parameters are openly displayed for each milk product.
How do I register as a Dairy Partner?
Click on "Dairy Partner" on the Sign In page, select "Create Account", provide your farm address, delivery radius, and FSSAI license. Once vetted by our admin team, your dairy will go live on the map.
